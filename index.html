From the workbench

Built because I needed it.

I teach high school physics, and most of the projects here start with something I wish I had for my own classroom. Sometimes it’s a better way to represent a physics idea; sometimes it’s a tool to make planning or assessment easier. So I build it, use it, and keep tinkering with it.

This site is where I share the ones that might be useful to other teachers, too.
